Ask about this project

What it is

I start with my own five uncertainties for the economy and five for economics as a field. Then I will ask scholars at Harvard and MIT to name theirs, one to one, before they see anyone else's. After each conversation I distil: duplicates merge, the scholars' wording stays, and the set moves on to the next conversation, where it is reworded, merged and extended. The result is a set of questions crafted together, not a pile of forecasts.

It follows the logic of a Future Atlas: uncertainties first, then maps, and signals only as a second stage. A small app supports the conversations; it does not replace them.

The first conversations are still to come. Harvard and MIT are where they will take place; neither institution sponsors the project.

What it will produce

  • One shared set of questions, worded together, which every scholar reads once and can comment on
  • Two maps, one for the economy and one for economics as a field, each spanned by the two uncertainties that matter most and move independently of each other
  • As a second stage, signals for each axis: forecast questions with a date and a clear criterion
