America 2030 Future Atlas

Where could the United States stand in 2030? Four scenarios, eight signals that show early which way the country is heading, and a forecast of the 2026 midterms behind the first two.

America 2030 Future AtlasMidterms dashboardCode on GitHubAsk about this project

What it is

Two open questions span four scenarios for 2030: do checks and balances hold, and how fast does AI change the economy? Eight signals show early which way the country is heading; the first two resolve on Election Day, 3 November 2026.

Behind them sits a forecast of the 2026 House and Senate elections, built for the forecasting contest of a course at the Harvard Kennedy School (API-232: Computational Policy Analysis). Every race combines polls, prediction-market prices and fundamentals, and all races are simulated together 20,000 times.
